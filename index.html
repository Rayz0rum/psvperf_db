PSVita Performance Database

A community-driven collection of recommended clocks, framerate limits, and resolutions for PSVita games.

Overview

Most PSVita games are primarily CPU-bound. This means that decreasing resolution often leads to diminishing returns in performance, and that some games may not suffer from drastically worse performance if you increase its resolution. Lowering resolution may help in GPU-heavy titles, but in many cases the CPU remains the bottleneck.

Tools Explained

  • PSVShell: A plugin that allows overclocking the Vita’s CPU/GPU/BUS/XBAR components, and monitoring system performance.
  • PSVShell Plus: An enhanced version of PSVShell directed situated in the Quick Menu, with additional stability and current-draw tracking.
  • VitaGrafix: A plugin that lets you change the framerate limit and resolution (whether internal resolution, or framebuffer) per game, reducing GPU load and improving framerate stability.
